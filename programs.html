What we do

Three parts to our work: teaching, school camps, and awareness.

Teaching

Classes and workshops on physics and astrophysics for students. We aim to explain ideas clearly, show them with simple experiments where we can, and welcome every question.

Topics we are planning around:

  • Motion, forces and gravity
  • Light, and what it tells us about stars
  • How stars are born, live and die
  • Planets, galaxies and the scale of the universe

Schedule, age groups and venues are still being planned.

School camps

We want to bring physics and astronomy to students at their own schools, through camps with hands-on activities and sky observation.

If you are a teacher or school and would like to talk about hosting a camp, please write to us.

Camp format, length and requirements are still being worked out.

Awareness

Talks and small public events that make science easier to approach for people of any age, including parents and families.

No events have been planned yet. They will be listed here when they are.
