About CEPHEI

Who we are and why we are doing this.

Our aim

We teach physics and astrophysics to students, run camps at schools, and raise awareness about science wherever we can. We want people to see that these subjects are not only for a few, and that asking questions about the universe is a good place to start.

The name

Cepheus is a constellation in the northern sky. Its star Delta Cephei brightens and fades in a steady rhythm, and astronomers use stars like it to measure distances in space. We liked the idea of a group that helps people find their way around.
