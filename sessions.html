Three kinds of sessions

All of them are free and open to anyone. Pick one and read the details, then join us when you are ready.

Dark-sky camps

Overnight telescope sessions away from city lights. We cover finding your way around the sky and taking photos with a phone, and there is a campfire at the end.

Build-it workshops

Afternoon labs where you build something and take it home: a spectroscope from a CD, a water rocket, or a small orbit simulation in code.

Pop-up science

Solar telescopes and tabletop physics demos set up in school yards, parks and plazas. Walk up whenever you like.
